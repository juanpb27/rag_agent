# Draiver - Company Knowledge Base

## Overview

Draiver is an AI-powered vehicle logistics platform that connects businesses with a nationwide network of over 20,000 fully insured and background-checked drivers. Founded in 2013 and headquartered in Overland Park, Kansas, Draiver delivers car transport solutions that are fast, efficient, and scalable.

The company provides services to OEMs, fleet managers, dealerships, auto auctions, rental agencies, and consumers. Its patented software enables intelligent dispatching, route optimization, real-time tracking, and advanced analytics, delivering substantial cost and time savings.

Draiver's flexible platform adapts to a variety of operational needs—from personal vehicle relocation to high-volume commercial transfers—helping businesses scale logistics without owning assets or maintaining internal fleets.

---

## Core Solutions

### 1. **Draiver NOW**
- On-demand mobile app for personal or business vehicle relocation.
- Fast booking, live vehicle tracking, instant driver matching, and delivery confirmation.
- No contracts or annual fees; pay-per-use model.

### 2. **Draiver ELITE**
- Fully managed vehicle movement solution for businesses.
- White-glove logistics with trained and insured drivers, dedicated dispatchers, and tailored SLAs.
- Ideal for fleet operators, rental companies, and dealership chains.

### 3. **Draiver IQ**
- AI-based logistics SaaS for enterprise clients.
- Enables automated route generation, driver scoring, real-time analytics, and API integration with internal systems.
- Offers customizable dashboards and KPI monitoring.

### 4. **Draiver FLEX**
- Modular solution for businesses with unique logistics challenges.
- Supports mixed-use fleets, subcontracting workflows, custom pricing tiers, and cross-border logistics.
